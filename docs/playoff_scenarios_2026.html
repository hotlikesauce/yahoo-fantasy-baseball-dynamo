The Tiebreaker

Teams finishing level on points are separated by the categories they won head-to-head — add up the categories each took in the weeks those two actually played, week 22 included if they meet in it. This is not the same as who won more of those matchups: two meetings of 7–5 and 4–8 look like a 1–1 split, but on categories they are 11–13. The categories decide it.
