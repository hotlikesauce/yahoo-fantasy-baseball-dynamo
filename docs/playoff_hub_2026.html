How the Prediction Works

A week is drawn whole. To play one matchup, the model picks a week each team actually had and scores its twelve categories against the other's. Drawing the whole week matters: a team's ERA, WHIP and K/9 all move together with how its starters went, and a model that drew each category on its own would happily deal a team its best ERA of the year alongside its fewest strikeouts — a week that has never happened and never will.

The innings rule is simulated, not smoothed over. A drawn week under 50 IP forfeits all five pitching categories, exactly as Yahoo scores it. Managers who habitually run short carry that risk into their odds rather than getting credit for tidy rate stats posted on 35 innings.

Ties go to the better seed, which is the league's rule. A 6–6 week is not rare here, so it is worth knowing which way it falls before it happens.

What it does not know. Injuries, call-ups, who is actually setting a lineup in September, and the two-start weeks that decide most pitching categories. A two-week window is eleven weeks of noise short of meaning anything on its own, which is why it is shown next to the season rather than instead of it.
